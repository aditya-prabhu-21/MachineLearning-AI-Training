This project focuses on identifying customers who accepted a driving coupon versus customers who don't. Below is a summary of my findings: 

Overall, coupon acceptance is strongly influenced by visit frequency and timing. For bar coupons, drivers who frequent bars more than once a month, 
particularly those aged 25–30, have the highest acceptance rates, while marital status and occupation appear to have little impact. For coffeehouse coupons, 
acceptance rises significantly for frequent visitors, with the highest response (81%) when coupons are sent around 10 AM, and longer drive times reduce 
likelihood of redemption. These findings suggest that targeting frequent visitors at optimal times is the most effective strategy for maximizing coupon 
acceptance, while certain demographics like marital status or occupation may be less relevant.
